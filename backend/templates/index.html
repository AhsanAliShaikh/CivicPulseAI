How AI Transforms Municipal Response

Traditional civic reporting relies on manual triage and fragmented departments. CivicPulse AI automates intelligence from submission to resolution.

Smart Categorization

NLP automatically extracts issue context, classifying reports into Roads, Sanitation, Public Safety, or Electrical departments instantly.

Priority Prediction

Assesses hazard severity, location impact, and public risk metrics to route urgent safety hazards ahead of routine maintenance.

Multimodal Vision

Analyzes photo evidence using Gemini Vision API to verify pothole dimensions, structural damage, or overflowing waste.

Duplicate Clustering

Groups duplicate reports from nearby residents into single actionable dispatch tickets, saving administrative overhead.
